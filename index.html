Who I am

My approach to learning is fundamentally applied. When a concept or challenge captures my attention — whether a software architecture, a market dynamic, or a physical system — I engage with it directly. Reading is a starting point; building is where understanding solidifies.

My background sits deliberately at the intersection of business and technology. I built my foundation in business administration at Bologna, then expanded into innovation management and agribusiness — two domains where technology is actively redefining competitive advantage. The objective was fluency on both sides: strategic and operational.

My most recent professional experience was at PwC Advisory, coordinating a multimillion-euro automotive IT consulting program. In parallel, I pursue personal technical projects — agentic AI systems, a home hydroponic farm, software tools — treating each as a structured opportunity to deepen applied capability.

I believe the most effective contributors to innovation are those who can move fluently between strategic thinking and technical execution — and hold both frames simultaneously.
